# Ideas

Concepts at any stage of maturity, from a rough spark to something ready to plan.

## Maturity tags

- `#idea/spark` — a thought worth not losing. May be wrong.
- `#idea/exploring` — being investigated; evidence accumulating.
- `#idea/ready` — understood well enough to plan. Graduates to a `[[Planning/...]]` note, which links back here.

## Naming

State the idea, not its category: `Usage-based onboarding emails.md`, not `Growth ideas.md`.

## Rules

- **An idea is not a commitment.** Recording one costs nothing and settles nothing.
- Say what would have to be **true** for it to work, and what would kill it. That is what moves it between stages.
- **Check it against the binding documents early.** An idea that violates a principle is dead on arrival regardless of how good it is commercially — say so in the note and keep it as a record of the road not taken.
- When an idea graduates, link the plan and mark it. When one dies, keep it and record why.
