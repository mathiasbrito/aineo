# Learnings

Reusable knowledge extracted from work: a pattern, a non-obvious fix, a technique, a trap. **Atomic — one concept per note.**

## Naming

The **claim itself**, as a sentence: `A mutation that does not reach the running system proves nothing.md`. A title that states the finding is findable by search and readable in a link. `Testing notes.md` is neither.

## Required content

- **The claim**, in one or two sentences, up front.
- **Why it is true** — the mechanism, not just the observation.
- **How it showed up here** — the concrete case, with `file:line` or a commit.
- **Where it applies again** — the generalisation, and its limits.
- Tags and links to the `[[Projects/aineo]]` and `[[Sessions/...]]` it came from.

## Rules

- Extract a learning when the knowledge would help someone **who was not in the session** — otherwise it belongs in the session note.
- If a learning turns out to be wrong, **fix or delete it**. A wrong learning is worse than a missing one; it will be trusted.
- Keep it general enough to reuse, specific enough to verify.
- A learning the orchestrator writes from a wave is reviewed by a `records` reviewer before it merges — every sentence resting on an external fact names the source it claims.
