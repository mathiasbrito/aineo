## What was done

- **`plugin/aineo.lua`.** The per-session state and functions of the file (`panes_session`, `claimed`, `claimed_by_id`, `start_confirmed`, `claude_terminal`, `session_read_in_flight`, the follows, the claims, the start, the layout's opening, the switch and the join) became `make_tab(homes, key, on_bound)`, a tab over the five homes it is given. Above it the file holds what the editor shares: `tab_list`, `last_used_tab`, the handlers of `aineo.editors` (`FocusGained`, `TabEnter`, `VimLeavePre`, the switch told by another editor), `new_tab()`, `route_report()`, `acting_tab()`, `hold_tab()`, `close_tab()` and the `TabClosed` handler, `open_new_tab()`, `jump_to_tab()` and `claim_in_current_tab()`.
- **The first tab** is made of the homes the modules themselves are (`require('aineo.layout')` and the others), so every caller and suite of today stands; every tab after it of the `new()` copies, keyed `2`, `3`… by the order tabs were made. The first tab writes its entry with no `tab`, as today.
- **Keys and commands.** `:Aineo tab new` (`<Plug>(aineo-tab-new)`, `\T`, `<C-\>T`), `:Aineo tab {n}` for any n and `<Plug>(aineo-tab-1)` … `(aineo-tab-9)`, `\1` … `\9` and their twins; completion offers `new` and each tab page's number; any other word is refused with one error.
- **Routing.** The report home's router (`set_report_router`) is given at the first tab: a report goes to the tab whose Claude Code has the offer's token and whose panes follow its session, else a tab whose panes follow the session, else, offered `at_start`, the tab whose Claude Code has the token; a report of no known session goes to the token's tab (A293). An action acts on the tab whose tab page is current, or on the aineo tab used last, moving there; `:Aineo claim {id}` acts in the current tab, or moves to the tab that runs the session.
- **Entries.** Each tab writes its own entry, keyed by the tab; the entered tab's entry is written again at `TabEnter` (it is marked used); quitting removes every entry of the editor.
- **Closing.** A tab page that Neovim closes is found at `TabClosed` (`aineo.tabs`' `closed()`): the tab leaves `tab_list` first, its entry comes off the list, then its Claude Code is stopped as quitting stops it, its terminal wiped, its claim let go, its draft saved, and its layout, changes pane, Report and Claude home let go. The first tab leaves its homes, its terminal included, as they are, and is the tab the next tab opened is made of.
- **Titles.** The tabs home holds each aineo tab with the session's name; aineo's tab line is drawn while `'tabline'` is empty; a dictionary watcher on `b:aineo_session_name` of each Claude terminal has `:redrawtabline` run when the name changes; `require('aineo').tab_title(n)` gives a title.
- **Homes (A258, A293).** `let_go()` on the claude, report, draft and changes copies; the report copy takes a report of no known session when `options.at_start`. Their tests are in the copies' own suites.
- **Health and help.** `:checkhealth aineo` lists `\T`, `\1`–`\9` and their twins and gains a *Tab line* section; the help gains *Tabs*, `:Aineo-tab`, the `<Plug>` and key entries, the health lines and *Several tabs* in the limits.

## Decisions and spec conflicts

- **The first tab's homes after it closes (A261).** Reused, not retired. The amendment said retired; four pins of the entry suites (the exited terminal reshown by `:Aineo report` after `:tabonly`, the hidden line numbers kept after `1tabclose`, the layout's buffer names) stand on reuse, and the homes' module copies cannot be let go anyway. Recorded in the correction to the brief's amendment.
- **O8 against the brief's item 1, a spec conflict decided, not chosen silently.** O8 has every tab start in the folder; item 1 has every entry suite of today pass unchanged. A single-tab Neovim that does `:cd` and restarts Claude Code resumes that directory's own session, and four suites pin it (`test_entry_claude_name`, `test_entry_claude_resume`, `test_entry_session_handover` ×2, `test_entry_session_switch`). The first tab keeps `getcwd()`, every tab after it starts in `places.working_directory`. If the orchestrator rules every start in the folder, those pins move.
- **O6, rule 3.** The token's tab takes an `at_start` offer whatever its panes follow; its home decides. `test_entry_claim`'s case of a report kept with the directory's records while a claim holds the panes elsewhere needs it.
- **The first tab's entry has no key.** The entry suites read the file by the address alone.
- **The edge `aineo` → `aineo.tabs`** (`tab_title()`'s lazy require) is not in the modularity skill's direction table, nor is `aineo.tabs` in its module table. Reported; the require is inside the function.
- **The boundary** was widened, as the dispatch and A258 state, to `lua/aineo/report/` (A293's rule) and the four homes' `let_go()`; `tests/helpers/entry.lua`'s `USAGE` was edited, not added to, since it pins the subcommands' list: a helper pin that moved.
- **`plugin/aineo.lua` is now about 2500 lines.** The brief's boundary kept the per-tab composition in the composition root. A home of its own (a `session` or `tab` home between the root and the homes) would be the modular shape; proposed, not done.
