## What was done

- **The setting.** `office.idle_ms` (180000; a whole number of at least 1, or `false`), validated with the others and told in the help, the README and the health check.
- **The countdown** (`office/countdown.lua`, `office/init.lua`). A new office counts the user's idleness: one wait at a time, through a `schedule_after` option (a timer of the editor's loop by default, a recording stand-in in the cases that are not about real time). `settle()` is the one place that decides: with the cursor in the right side no countdown and no card; otherwise the countdown runs; when it has run out the office shows as soon as there is a right side and 'termguicolors' is on. It is called on `WinEnter`, `WinResized` and `VimResized`; `TabEnter` and a report coming in start the countdown again. A hidden office holds that one timer and a few autocommands; the frame timer, the tracker and the watch exist only while it shows. The settings are read only when a countdown is to start.
- **The engine homes** are required when the office first shows, so making an office for every tab loads none of them (pinned).
- **'termguicolors' off.** `show_now()` warns once with the reason and shows nothing; the countdown running out says nothing. `:checkhealth aineo` has an Office section: an error with advice when it is off, and when the office shows by itself.
- **Tabs.** `TabLeave` stops the frame timer, the tracker and the follower of the office in the tab page left; `TabEnter` starts them again, the follower's replay of the tail making the scene catch up.
- **Too small** (`office/too_small.lua`). Below 60 by 24 cells the office draws one line saying so, cut to the width, and asks the scene for nothing.
- **Report cards** (`office/cards.lua`). A line of text per report, drawn over the scene's bottom right corner: `● <status> · <summary>`, on one line, cut to the width, the newest at the bottom, three at most and `+N more` above, in `AineoOfficeCardDone`, `…Progress`, `…Problem` and `…More` (default links to the diagnostic groups); the dot of `blocked` and `failed` blinks every half second. The cards are kept until the cursor enters the right side. A report that comes in while the cursor is in the right side and the office is hidden keeps none (the Report is where the user is); one that comes in while the office shows over the cursor does. A report starts the countdown again and wakes nothing.
- **`permission_wait(agent)`** goes to the tracker as `permission_asked`; it ends at the agent's next transcript line (the tracker's rule, pinned end to end); a wait told while the office is hidden or paused is not kept. The entry's guard for an office without the method is gone.
- **The entry.** The office of a tab is made when its layout is bound to a tab page (`opening.lua`'s `hold_tab()`), let go when the tab is released (`tab.lua`), told each report the tab's Report took, and each permission wait. Because the office is made earlier than its first show, what it read at its first show is asked later: the classes of commands come with its settings, and the packs' folders are a function asked at each show.
- **Help and README.** The setting, the countdown, 'termguicolors', tabs, the small window, `aineo-office-cards` and its four highlight groups.

## The review round (A588)

The review of the pull request found eight items; each was built test first. The office takes a `transcript` option, asked each time it starts following (each show, whichever way, and each return to its tab page), in place of `follow_session()`: the office that showed itself followed no transcript or a stale one, and its failing case is an idle-shown office that never types. A wait the office is told is kept per agent with its wall-clock time (64 agents at most) and handed to the tracker each time the office starts running, unless the replayed tail shows the agent went on after it (the start of the call that asked aside); the hidden, paused and closed-and-shown-again cases and a `wall_clock` option pin both sides of that comparison. `let_go()` sets a flag `settle()` honours, so a tab page that closes leaves no countdown. A countdown that runs out after the setting was turned off no longer shows the office. M10 (an office that showed itself waits a new countdown when closed without the cursor entering the right side) and M11 (showing on request stops the countdown) are pinned with the reviewer's separating cases, and M17 is killed by an `expect.no_error` assertion. Entering the office's own window leaves the cursor in the first window it covers and forgets the cards. The cards case closes the float as production does.

## Reported, not chosen

- **The cards cannot come from `routing.route_report`.** The plan names it, but the router is called with a report's session and the offer's options, not with the report, and before the home decides whether it takes it. The report home's environment gained an optional `on_report_received`, called with each report after the Report shows it; the entry hands it to the tab's office. This touches `aineo.report`, which the brief did not name; the alternative was a third parameter of the router, which would still not tell whether the home took the report.
- **The scene's picture has no status list height.** The cards read an optional `status_list_height` of the picture (none: no status list). T129 draws the status list and should give the picture that field, or the name should change here.
- **The camera.** §1 asks the office to follow the agent in focus between the floor plan's smallest size and 60 by 24; that is the scene's. The office draws one line below 60 by 24 and the scene's picture above it, whatever the scene does with a small size.
- **The countdown restarts on entering a tab page.** The plan says nothing; an office whose tab page was left would otherwise show the moment the user came back.
- **Plan §1 says the cards stay until the office closes; A570 says until the user enters the right side.** A570 was followed.
- **A late permission wait.** A wait the editor delivers after the call's result line was read stays until the agent's next line (the tracker's rule). Pinned as the tracker has it; nothing in the transcript tells the answer came first.
- **`termguicolors` in the suites.** A headless child has it off; the office suites turn it on in the child. The small-window rule made the suites that show the office size their child at 160 by 40.
- **Health.** The Office section holds `termguicolors` and the idle time; the packs found and their problems are T134's.
