## 5. The web-link rule's table, run through a throwaway reference (rule.lua; not aineo code)

A link starts with http:// or https://, the scheme in any case, at the start of the text or after a character
that is not a letter or digit; it runs to whitespace, <, >, " or a backtick; trailing . , ; : ! ? ' " are left
out, and a trailing ), ] or } that has no opening partner inside the link; a link is at least one character
after the ://. Result per line (the link, [first byte, end byte)):
